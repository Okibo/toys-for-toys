## 2. Legal Basis for Processing

### 2.1 GDPR Article 6 - Lawfulness of Processing

We process your personal data based on one or more of the following legal bases:

**Article 6(1)(a) - Explicit Consent**
- You have given clear, unambiguous consent before data collection
- Consent is freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous
- Consent is as easy to withdraw as to give
- Applies to: Behavioral analytics, marketing communications, optional profiling

**Article 6(1)(b) - Contract Performance**
- Processing is necessary to perform our contract with you
- Without this data, we cannot provide the toy exchange service
- Applies to: Account creation, toy listings, exchanges, ticket management, messaging

**Article 6(1)(f) - Legitimate Interests**
- Processing is necessary for legitimate interests pursued by us or a third party
- Your rights do not override these legitimate interests
- Applies to: Fraud prevention, security, system improvement, legal compliance

### 2.2 GDPR Article 8 - Children's Data Protection

If you are under 16 years old (or the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction):
- Your parent or legal guardian must provide verifiable parental consent
- We require parental email verification
- We limit data collection to only what is necessary
- We do not use your data for targeted advertising (except service improvement)
- We implement additional safeguards for your data protection

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.

## 7. Your Rights Under GDPR

### 7.1 Right to Access (Article 15)

You have the right to obtain confirmation whether we hold your personal data and to receive a copy. To exercise this right:
- Visit Settings → Privacy → Download My Data
- Or email: dpo@toy-for-toy.example
- Response time: Within 30 calendar days

**What you will receive:**
- Complete copy of all personal data we hold
- Information about processing purposes
- Details about recipients
- Retention periods for each data category
- Format: JSON or CSV

### 7.2 Right to Rectification (Article 16)

You have the right to correct inaccurate personal data. You can:
- Update your profile information directly in Settings
- Request corrections for historical data via dpo@toy-for-toy.example
- Response time: Within 30 calendar days

### 7.3 Right to Erasure (Article 17)

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data ("Right to be Forgotten"). You can:
- Request deletion in Settings → Account → Delete Account
- Or email: dpo@toy-for-toy.example with subject line "Data Deletion Request"
- Response time: Account deletion complete within 30 days

**Exceptions:** We may retain data when:
- Required by law (tax, accounting records)
- Necessary for dispute resolution (1 year after deletion)
- Necessary for security and fraud prevention (1 year)
- Required for consent audit trail (3 years per GDPR Article 7)

**Consequences of deletion:**
- Account and all profile information permanently deleted
- Toy listings removed
- Access to the Platform revoked
- Cannot re-register with same email (for 30 days)

### 7.4 Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18)

You have the right to restrict how we process your data. When restricted:
- We retain your data but limit processing
- Your data is not deleted
- Restricted data is marked and processed only for limited purposes
- To exercise: Email dpo@toy-for-toy.example

### 7.5 Right to Data Portability (Article 20)

You have the right to receive your personal data and transmit it to another controller. You can:
- Download your data via Settings → Privacy → Download My Data
- Data format: JSON (machine-readable)
- Response time: Within 30 calendar days
- No fee charged

### 7.6 Right to Object (Article 21)

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can:
- Opt out of behavioral analytics in Settings → Privacy
- Opt out of marketing emails in email preferences
- Restrict processing for specific purposes
- To object: Email dpo@toy-for-toy.example

### 7.7 Right to Withdraw Consent (Article 7)

You have the right to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal is:
- As easy as providing consent

## 13. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

### 13.1 Fraud Detection

We use automated decision-making for fraud detection:
- **Purpose:** Prevent ticket fraud and account abuse
- **Basis:** Article 6(1)(f) - Legitimate interests
- **Mechanism:** Automated analysis of transaction patterns
- **Human Review:** Cases flagged for manual review before action taken
- **Right to Explanation:** You can request explanation of automated decisions
- **Right to Human Review:** You can request manual review before account restriction

### 13.2 Matching Algorithm

Our exchange matching algorithm:
- Matches toys based on condition, category, and user preferences
- **Not profiling:** No behavioral profiles created
- **No tracking:** Does not track your broader behavior
- **No automated decisions:** Humans make final matching decisions
- **Transparent:** You see how matches are recommended

### 13.3 Limitations on Automated Processing

We do NOT use automated decision-making for:
- Denying service based solely on automated analysis
- Creating persistent user profiles
- Micro-targeting for advertising (except aggregate analytics)
- Restricting access without human review
