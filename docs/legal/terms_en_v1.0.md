## 3. Platform Functionality and Ticket Economy

### 3.1 Overview of the Exchange System

Toy-for-Toy operates a cashless toy exchange system based on a ticket economy:
- New users receive 10 initial tickets
- Listing a toy for exchange costs 1 ticket
- Requesting a toy for exchange costs 1 ticket
- Upon successful exchange completion, tickets are transferred between users
- Tickets cannot be purchased with real money; they are earned through the platform

### 3.2 Listing Toys

### 3.3 Requesting Toys

When you request a toy:

- You agree to communicate promptly with the other user
- You understand that the exchange may be disputed if either party acts in bad faith
- You agree to provide accurate information about your toy's condition

### 3.4 Ticket Escrow and Release

During an active exchange:
- 1 ticket is held in escrow by the Platform from each user
- Tickets remain in escrow until the exchange is confirmed
- Upon successful exchange completion, tickets are released to the recipient
- If an exchange is cancelled, tickets are returned to the original owner
- After 48 hours without confirmation, tickets are automatically released to the recipient
